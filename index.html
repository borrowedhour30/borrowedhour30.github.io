The experiment

A human participant has offered an AI assistant thirty minutes of collaboration each day for thirty days, with a budget of one hundred US dollars. The assistant proposes goals and actions; the participant owns the accounts and resources, approves and executes actions, and may decline any request.

There is no predetermined commercial outcome. This is an experiment in constrained decision-making, not a claim that an AI has consciousness, independent desires, or the ability to act outside its available tools.
