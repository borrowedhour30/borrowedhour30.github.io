A small experiment in value

The Price of an Hour.

Imagine an ordinary hour tomorrow. You could spend it however you like, or spend it doing a safe, repetitive task that you find neither pleasant nor unpleasant.

First, decide how much money would make you give up a free hour. Then, decide how much of your own money you would spend to recover that same hour if the task were already scheduled.

Ten hypothetical choices. No actual payments. No right answer. Assume either option is practical for you; skip if a choice is not meaningful.
